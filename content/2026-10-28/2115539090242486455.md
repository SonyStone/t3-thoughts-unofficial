Theo quotes a video of models being re-ranked as the Artificial Analysis leaderboard moves from version 4.3.2 to 5. Artificial Analysis is an independent service that combines model benchmarks into one index.

His comment is that it's a terrible day to be someone who says open models are as good as Opus 5.5. The implication is that the gap between Opus 5.5 and open models grew in the new version of the index, but he gives no numbers, and the video couldn't be watched. In replies that same night he added that the gap to open models was two to three months for a while, and now it has grown again.
