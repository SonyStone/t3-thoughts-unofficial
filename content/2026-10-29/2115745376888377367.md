A user asks what Theo thinks about a specialized harness for scientific research and R&D, with tools built for that. The argument is that current AI apps are almost entirely focused on coding.

Theo replies briefly: if you're doing research and have ideas for things current harnesses don't do, you "10000%" should build that. He doesn't say how such a harness should be different.

How to approach it he explained later in the same thread, when the user doubted their engineering skills: audit a week of work with the agent, then ask "what can we build" (see the neighboring post with the step-by-step process).
