Theo asks his audience whether T3 Code should take on "harness configuration". He lists three examples:
- a skill manager (skills are markdown instructions an agent loads depending on the task);
- an MCP server marketplace (MCP is the protocol for connecting external tools to an agent);
- global management of AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md files, the standing instructions for agents.

It's a question, not an announcement, and the post makes no decision. In the replies he clarified that his own default is Opus 5.5 with nothing extra.
