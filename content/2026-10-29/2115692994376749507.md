grim pushes back: love you, Theo, but a couple of "software factories" have had this for a while. That's the name for platforms where agents pick up tasks on their own and turn out PRs.

Theo spells out the difference: "Not with MCP that works with things like Grok Bot across a whole fleet." So he isn't claiming to be the first to run agents on several machines. His claim is narrower. Control over all the machines is exposed as an MCP server, and a third-party agent or bot can use it.
