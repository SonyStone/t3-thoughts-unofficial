Chris Boulton asks why there should be a separate feature for branching a thread when you can just keep writing in the same one: "also can you..", "where are you at?", "start a new thread with..". In Chris's experience this works well with frontier models and even with open ones.

Theo agrees. It feels weird to him too to build a thread split when you can just ask. He expects that if they implement it, it will stop mattering within a month, tops. He's mostly doing it so people stop asking.

Judging by nearby posts, this is about side chats, which branch a conversation off into a separate panel. One user has already wired them into a T3 Code nightly build on their own. It's a rare case of Theo saying outright that he's building a feature because people keep asking for it, not because he thinks it's needed.
