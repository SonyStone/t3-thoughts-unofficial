Younes writes that they can't think of a single case where a UI for talking to agents beats VM + tmux + Claude Code.

Theo replies that tmux is incredible until you need more than nine panes. Most likely he means quick switching with the number keys, of which there are only nine or ten. With a dozen agents running in parallel, keeping track of them in tmux gets hard. That's my reading, the post contains only the line itself.

It's the most concrete argument against terminals in the thread under his post: you need a GUI once you have a lot of agents.
