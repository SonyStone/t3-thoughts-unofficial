This continues the conversation with a researcher who wanted their own "research harness", but wrote that they're more of a researcher than an engineer and couldn't build anything useful. A harness here means everything wrapped around the model: tools, prompts and the agent loop, as in Claude Code, Codex or T3 Code.

Theo suggests not starting by building, and getting there through an audit of your own work instead:
1. Work in the existing harnesses for a week.
2. Ask Claude: "audit all the things we did last week, look for holes in your process and things that we got stuck on".
3. Read the list, suggest changes, help the agent sort the problems into categories.
4. Ask: "Okay, what can we build to address these issues? Would a custom harness make sense here?"
5. Iterate.

With this approach you hardly need engineering skills: the agent does the building, and the person has to know their own problems. That's my interpretation, Theo doesn't put it that way himself. The same move is in his video about prompts: ask the agent first, then build.
