To the jab "says the guy selling the gui", Theo answers with sarcasm about himself: this vibe coder Theo will never make any money on this, he forgot to close source the codebase. And he links the repository.

The [pingdotgg/t3code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code) repository describes T3 Code as a control panel for agent harnesses on your own machine. There are iOS and Android apps, a web version and an Electron desktop app. It supports Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Grok Build, OpenCode and Google Antigravity, and each one has to be installed and logged into separately. The license is MIT, with about 31.4k stars at the time of checking. The README warns that the project is very early and mostly isn't accepting outside contributions yet.
