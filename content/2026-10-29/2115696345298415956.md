A new video announcement. Theo writes that he's been "claudemaxxing" a bit too hard lately, squeezing as much as he can out of Claude. He needed more compute, more accounts, more guardrails, and now he just needs more sleep. He calls the video a chaotic spiral into hill climbing, which means improving a system step by step against a measurable metric. It's the start of a series, and in his words it only gets weirder from here.

He quotes a viewer's post noticing that the video mentions [boat.dev](https://boat.dev). That's a cloud service of persistent Linux VMs (sandboxes) for agents, with SSH, Docker, a virtual desktop, forks from snapshots, and control through a CLI, an HTTP API and an SDK. Billing is per second, with a $20 monthly minimum.

A frame from the video shows a whiteboard with a calculation: "$500–800 of sub usage", the question of how much he spent on compute, and "$2,000 of blacksmith compute" (Blacksmith is a CI runner service). Boat.dev is written below.
