Chase Robbins asks for built-in boat.dev support in T3 Code, so that each worktree becomes its own sandbox. boat.dev offers cloud Linux VMs for agents with SSH, Docker and snapshots, which you can manage through a CLI and an API.

Theo says no: "No. Just tell your agent to use boat." Since the service has a CLI, the agent can spin up a sandbox and work in it by itself. There's no need for a separate button in T3 Code. It's the same idea as in his video about prompts: a lot of what looks like a feature is solved by one sentence to the agent.
